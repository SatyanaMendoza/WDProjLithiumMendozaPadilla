Description:
    Welcome to the world of Stardew Valley, a game where you can take care of your own farm, decorate your home, customize your own character, meet loads of new people, and so much more! With the endless activities, one may never run out of things to do here. This website aims to introduce Stardew Valley to new potential players and help them in gaining new insights about it. This website will cover information regarding the game’s backstory, the valley, the bachelors, bachelorettes, and the other characters.

Webpages:

Home Page:  The home page, the first page to be seen upon entering the website, includes the website's description.  It also includes buttons leading to the 'Backstory', 'The Valley', 'Bachelors', 'Bachelorettes', and 'Other Characters' pages.

Backstory Page: This page includes a description on the game's backstory and a picture of the scene in the game.  It also includes a menu on the top right corner.  When clicked, it reveals buttons that lead to the other webpages.
