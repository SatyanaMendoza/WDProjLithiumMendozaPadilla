-Evelyn-

Evelyn is a sweet old woman who lives on River Road with her husband George. She is in charge of the town's flowers. She has lived here her whole life, so she knows almost everyone and gives useful advice.
Birthday: Winter 20
Clinic Visit:
  • The second of each season
  • The 23rd of each season (with George)
Favorite Items:
  • Beet
  • Chocolate Cake
  • Diamond
  • Fairy Rose
  • Raisins
  • Stuffing
  • Tulip
Family:
  • George (Husband)
  • Alex (Grandson)
Address: 1 River Road

-George-

George is an old grumpy man in a wheelchair with a kind heart. He is a retired farmer and lives with his wife Evelyn and Grandson Alex on 1 River Road. Clara is the deceased daughter of George and the mother of Alex.
Birthday: Fall 24
Clinic Visit: The 23rd of each season
Favorite Items:

Family:

-Gunther-

Gunther is a villager who operates the Archaeology Center in Pelican Town. He doesn't accept friendship gifts, and instead accepts donations to the Museum. He'll reward players as they reach milestones in number of donations.
